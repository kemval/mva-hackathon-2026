## The finding: a mouse model of a real MVA patient's genotype is embryonic lethal, and the patient lived

**Sieben CJ, Jeganathan KB, Nelson GG, Sturmlechner I, Zhang C, van Deursen WH, Bakker B,
Foijer F, Li H, Baker DJ, van Deursen JM. "BubR1 allelic effects drive phenotypic
heterogeneity in mosaic-variegated aneuploidy progeria syndrome." *J Clin Invest*
2020;130(1):171–188. PMID 31738183, DOI 10.1172/JCI126863.** Open access (bronze); obtained
in full (20 pp) from the University of Groningen deposit after Europe PMC returned 404.

The paper set out to model an actual published MVA patient — **truncating + kinase-domain
missense**, the same allelic architecture as this proband. Verbatim section heading:

> "**Mice modeling MVA patient BUBR1^X753/L1012P die during early embryogenesis.**"

> "BUBR1 2211insGTTA, a mutant BUBR1 allele that results in a frameshift and yields an
> unstable truncated protein referred to as BUBR1^X753, has been identified in 2 biallelic MVA
> patients, one of which also inherits BUBR1 3035T>C, a mutant allele that encodes
> BUBR1^L1012P."

> "we genotyped a total of **39 E13.5 mice** from heterozygous intercrosses, but again **no
> BubR1^X753/L1002P mice were observed**… However, repeating the analysis at E3.5 yielded
> viable BubR1^X753/L1002P embryos."

> "**Following our unsuccessful attempt to model the BUBR1^X753/L1012P MVA patient in mice**,
> we asked whether the BubR1^H allele, which mimics the hypomorphic allele found in patients
> with monoallelic BUBR1 mutations, might produce viable offspring…"

**The human carrying that genotype was born and lived. The mouse carrying its equivalent dies
before E13.5.** The authors state the failure plainly and pivot to a different allele pair.

### Why this matters more than a single mouse result usually would

**1. It bounds the entire mouse tier of the Track 2 evidence base.** The viable model the
field uses — **BubR1^H/H** — pairs two hypomorphic alleles producing low wild-type protein.
No MVA patient has that genotype. Patients have truncating + missense (Suijkerbuijk 2010:
*"In patients with biallelic mutations, a missense mutation pairs with a truncating
mutation."*). So the mouse models underwriting the report's senescence, NAD⁺ and senolytic
arms model **a genotype that does not occur in this disease**, and the genotype that does
occur is not viable in mouse.

**2. It cannot be dismissed as a rival group's result.** The senior author is **Jan van
Deursen**, with **Darren Baker** — the same laboratory behind Baker 2011
(`literature/04b_...`), the BubR1 progeroid senescence-clearance work the report leans on.
This is the model-builders reporting the limits of their own models.

**3. The direction of the discordance is specific and counterintuitive.** Mouse is *more*
severe than human at the equivalent genotype. That means mouse lethality does not predict
human lethality — and, read the other way, a **mouse rescue does not license a human
expectation**. Both halves belong in the report.

### ⚠️ A numbering trap that will catch anyone searching "BubR1 1002"

| | residue 1002 | residue 1012 |
|---|---|---|
| **Human BUBR1** | **`p.Asn1002Lys` — this proband's variant** | `p.Leu1012Pro` — a published MVA allele |
| **Mouse BubR1** | `L1002P` — the mouse ortholog of **human L1012P** | — |

**Mouse `BubR1^L1002P` and human `BUBR1 N1002K` are different residues in different species
that share a number.** The paper uses both numbering systems in the same sentences. Task 1
already established that human L1012 sits 12.3 Å from human N1002 — genuinely adjacent, which
makes the confusion *more* likely, not less. Any future search on "BubR1 1002" must
disambiguate species before quoting.

### LIMITS — what this paper does NOT show

- **Mouse only.** No human data, no patient treated, nothing about therapy.
- It does **not** show that this proband's genotype is lethal — the opposite: the human with
  the analogous architecture survived, which is the entire point.
- The viable model (`BubR1^H/L1002P`) is a **mouse-specific allele pair**, not a patient
  genotype.
- Tumour work is in mice, including DMBA-induced lung tumours — a carcinogen challenge, not a
  spontaneous MVA cancer phenotype.
- It offers no human genotype–phenotype correlation, which is what Task 2 actually asked for.

### Evidence tier

**Mouse, in the BubR1 background specifically** — the highest-relevance animal tier available
in this project, and still mouse. Its principal use here is as a **limit on other mouse
evidence**, not as support for any claim about this child.

## Incidental finding relevant to Task 4

The AJMG 2025 review (Frattini et al., obtained in full) contains **zero mentions** of
"nephrocalcinosis", "renal" or "kidney" outside a single reference to Wilms tumour. Combined
with Kajii 1998 (renal **cysts** and Wilms, not nephrocalcinosis), this is **weak accumulating
evidence that nephrocalcinosis is not a recognised MVA feature** — but it is two sources, not
the systematic search Task 2 specifies, and must not be reported as an exhaustive negative.
