The skew doesn't track loss-of-function. It tracks whether oncology wants to inhibit the pathway. The corrected rule is diagnostic — and this child's gene is in the worst category.

Beat 4 · 1:48–2:12 · What we would not give him

The top hit of a keyword search is among the most contraindicated agents available

17-AAG

top hit of the best-known “drugs for aneuploidy” paper

Mutant BubR1 is an HSP90 client.

Block HSP90 → destroy the little protein this child still has.

Gates were written before the search. HSP90 inhibitors are also a recognised senolytic class — so “senolytic” is not a safe class label here, and the gate is applied agent by agent.

The NAD⁺ mechanism is inferred, not shown — the mouse work everyone cites used a different intervention in a different genotype. We don't blur supplement and medicine.
